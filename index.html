Our story

Scroll down to trigger scroll-depth events at 25%, 50%, 75% and 100%.

Fernhill started in a garage with a popcorn popper and a lot of burnt beans. Ten years later we roast on a 12-kilo drum and still taste every batch.

We buy directly from growers wherever we can, and pay well above market price for the coffees we love.

Our roasting style is light to medium: we want you to taste the fruit, the sugar and the place it came from.
